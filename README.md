# Incident Report: Online Purchase Fraud & Financial Recovery Case

## Overview
This document details a real-life online fraud incident that occurred on **February 7-8, 2025**, involving an advance payment fraud via social media messaging and mobile financial service (bKash), followed by a successful reporting, account freeze, and fund recovery process.

---

## Technical Cyber Security Terms

1. **Non-Delivery Scam / Advance-Fee Fraud:** A cybercrime where the seller promises a subscription/service (Turboscribe AI Lifetime access) in exchange for advance payment, but fails to deliver the promised service after receiving the payment.
2. **Social Engineering / Financial Scam:** Deceiving an individual into voluntarily sending money using false promises on social media platforms.
3. **MFS Fraud (Mobile Financial Services Fraud):** Fraudulent transactions executed through mobile wallet platforms (e.g., bKash).

---

## Incident Timeline & Details

### 1. Initial Contact & Negotiation
* **Platform:** Social Media Messaging (Facebook Messenger)
* **Fraudster Profile Name:** Abhay Verma / Abhay
* **Offered Service:** "Turboscribe AI" lifetime subscription
* **Agreed Amount:** 700 BDT
* **Payment Method:** bKash Send Money to `01886306691`

### 2. The Fraud Incident
* **Date:** February 7, 2025
* **Details:** 
  * The buyer sent BDT 700 to the provided bKash number (`01886306691`).
  * Transaction ID: `CB7BM5AQZU`
  * The seller promised delivery within 30 minutes.
  * After receiving the payment, the seller stopped responding, missed multiple audio calls, and failed to activate/provide the subscription.

---

## Action Taken & Resolution Process

### Step 1: Reporting & Account Freeze
* **Action:** Immediately called the official bKash Customer Care / Call Center.
* **Result:** Provided transaction details and requested a temporary account freeze/block on the fraudster's bKash number (`01886306691`).

### Step 2: Fraudster Contact & Recovery
* **Day 2:** The owner/user of the blocked bKash account contacted the victim to inquire about the frozen account.
* **Negotiation:** The victim confirmed initiating the official block due to the scam.
* **Pressure & Confession:** Throughout the afternoon, multiple calls were made from different phone numbers to pressure the victim. By evening, after answering, the perpetrator admitted to the fraud and agreed to refund the money.
* **Refund:** The perpetrator refunded the full BDT 700 to the victim.

### Step 3: Complaint Withdrawal
* **Final Action:** 1–2 days after receiving the full refund, the victim contacted bKash support to formally withdraw/cancel the complaint and unfreeze the account.

---

## Key Lessons & Mitigation Steps

* **Always verify third-party vendors:** Never buy digital subscriptions from unverified social media profiles.
* **Prompt Reporting:** Swiftly reporting fraudulent transactions to Mobile Financial Service (MFS) helplines increases the probability of freezing funds and recovering money.
* **Proof of Transaction:** Always retain screenshots of conversations, call logs, transaction IDs, and recipient details for law enforcement or customer support verification.
